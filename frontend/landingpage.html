LearnQuest: Adaptive Learning Management System

Learn with lessons that adapt to you.

How it works

From lesson to mastery

1

Your teacher uploads the lesson

Learning materials are added to your class as PDF or PowerPoint files.

2

AI summarises and writes the quiz

Lesson summaries and quiz questions are generated from that material, not from generic content.

3

Mastery is tracked per competency

Each answer updates a Bayesian Knowledge Tracing estimate of what you know, not just a quiz score.

4

Your next step adapts

Review, more practice, or harder questions, chosen from your current mastery.

Features

Three Things LearnQuest Does

Adaptive Quizzes

AI-generated quizzes whose difficulty follows your mastery of each competency.

Personalized Feedback

Explanations and hints for the questions you missed, plus QuestAI for help with your lessons.

Mastery Analytics

Per-competency progress for students, and class progress for teachers to see who needs support.
